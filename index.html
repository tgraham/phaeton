What you're getting

380 hp Cummins diesel

Rear-mounted 8.9L with 1,150 lb-ft of torque and an Allison 6-speed. Tows up to 10,000 lb.

Four slide-outs

Opposing slides open up the living area; the bedroom gets its own, with a king bed.

Bath and a half

Rear bath with a large glass shower and dual sinks, plus a half bath off the living area.

Residential kitchen

French-door refrigerator with ice and water, 3-burner gas cooktop, convection microwave, pull-out pantry.

Laundry, safe and central vac

Stacked washer and dryer in the bedroom closet with an in-wall safe beside it. A built-in InterVac central vacuum has an interior port and a hose long enough to reach the whole coach or the outside storage bays.

Room for everything

Slide-out basement trays, pass-through bays, under-bed storage, and drawers in the dinette and entry steps.
